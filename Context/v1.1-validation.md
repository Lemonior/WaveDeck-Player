## Changes

- Perceptual volume: 50% maps to −10 dB; gain changes ramp over 15 ms. Every slider supports click-to-position and double-click reset. EQ Default restores bands, preamp, stereo width, safe gain, enabled EQ and A/B bypass while preserving volume.
- URL label and URL/search placeholders align with their input centers.
- Next audio is prepared during the last 30 seconds. YouTube media rejections retry fresh resolution, then a compatible format, then a temporary yt-dlp playback cache. Manual preparation keeps existing audio playing; autoplay skips inaccessible tracks with concise errors.
- Batched 500-track pages, Load next / Load all, default total 10,000 and configurable ceiling 50,000. Playlist rows are virtualized. Download modes queue later pages while the player remains usable. List tools can download all loaded online tracks.
- Sequential background downloads support cancellation and retry. Finished files are reused by URL/folder/format/quality. Download & play starts after a file is saved and waits for queued files. Pending jobs do not automatically resume after closing; requeue remembered/reimported tracks to continue.
- Modern, brown/gold OldSchool and violet/cyan/pink Retro preview live and persist on Save. Integrated header supports drag, double-click maximize/restore, minimize and close. Spotify and cookie-file selection were removed.
- Sign in with Google opens a fresh app-owned Edge/Chrome browser. Connect reads only that browser's Google/YouTube session, then closes/removes its temporary profile. Windows DPAPI encrypts the saved session; temporary per-request cookies are deleted on completion. Existing personal browser profiles and passwords are not read. Disconnect deletes the saved connection.

## Practical limits

Actual Google sign-in, private/member/age-restricted media and listening quality require the user's interactive check. Browser mechanics were verified with synthetic session data. Account eligibility still governs access; YouTube may expire or restrict sessions. This is browser-session sign-in, not an OAuth permission grant.

The 10,000-entry checks used generated metadata/jobs. Live paging used two pages and downloading used one small public video; no 10,000-video media download was performed. Preloading reduces typical transitions but does not guarantee gapless output: network requests, rejection/fallback and pending downloads can still delay playback. Runtime testing used this Windows x64 workstation, build 10.0.26200.
