## Practical limits

Physical mouse gestures need a manual feel check; shared-desktop cursor injection was not reliable. Automated checks exercise the enlarged WPF hit area and drag-position mapping, including positions outside the rail. Taskbar/media commands were delivered as native messages to the test app's own window.

No real Google account or regular browser session was accessed by validation. Opening sign-in uses a standard Windows shell HTTPS link. The explicit Connect step can be limited by browser session protection, locking or profile choice. Private/member access and watch-history acceptance remain site-controlled interactive checks. Watch requests are optional and do not interrupt audio if rejected.

Songs come from published video chapter metadata; unidentified songs cannot be split reliably. Downloads are verified as individual audio segments; downloaded segments replay from zero. Network failures, new resolution and pending downloads can still delay transitions. Reduced interruptions are verified; strictly gapless output and audible listening quality are not claimed.
