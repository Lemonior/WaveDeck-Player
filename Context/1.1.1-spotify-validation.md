## Behavior

All three clients import public Spotify track, playlist and album metadata through public embed pages. Full links, locale-prefixed links, canonical Spotify URIs and recognized short-link redirects are handled without Spotify credentials. Redirects remain restricted to recognized Spotify hosts, responses are bounded, and metadata caches expire. No private account tokens, Spotify audio or preview files are used.

The queue retains Spotify titles, artists, available ordering and catalog URLs. Windows/Linux keep the chosen YouTube URL in `MatchedUrl`; Android resolves a virtual source carrying the catalog metadata, keeping the Spotify URL as the cache/queue identity. Matching runs when audio is needed, including bounded existing lookahead, rather than searching the entire playlist before import. It scores title coverage, artist evidence and duration, rejecting common mismatched recording versions and uncertain matches. A/B/EQ, seeking, transport, streaming, downloading and Download & play use the existing playback path. Spotify entries do not expand matched music-video chapters.

Source labels distinguish Spotify → YouTube. Original and matched source actions are available after matching; saved catalog links remain Spotify links. Both English and Brazilian Portuguese text explain the limitation that only Spotify's public embed list can be imported. Private playlists, local-only items and podcasts are unsupported; larger playlists can be incomplete. A match is best effort, not a guarantee of an identical recording. Use a direct YouTube URL for a particular version.
